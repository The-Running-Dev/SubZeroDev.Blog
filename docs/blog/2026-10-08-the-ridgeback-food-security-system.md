Bonnie has had meat.

Bonnie has had treats.

Bonnie would like the record to show that none of this has any fucking relevance to the bread currently in my hand.

<!-- truncate -->

She's a Ridgeback.

I'm trying to eat soup.

These two facts cannot apparently coexist peacefully.

The dog is beside me looking like she hasn't eaten since the fall of communism.

You had meat this morning.

Her face suggests this is an allegation she intends to dispute.

And treats.

No recollection.

More treats.

She would like to speak to somebody about the bread.

## Soup Tax

She gets some soup meat.

You might think that would settle the matter.

It establishes precedent.

Now she knows the soup contains something she wants, and I have demonstrated that transferring it to the dog is possible.

Any subsequent failure to do so is clearly a personal decision.

Then there's the bread.

I move. She follows.

The bread moves. She follows more closely.

It's difficult to enjoy childhood food memories when a large dog is conducting surveillance on every fucking mouthful.

And then she notices the pepper flakes.

## ABORT MISSION

A sniff.

Immediate retreat.

Wait a fucking minute.

This dog, who has spent the meal pursuing soup and bread with the determination of a debt collector, has suddenly remembered urgent business elsewhere.

I show her the packet again.

Off she goes.

Like it's fucking poison.

Oh.

**So there are things you don't want to eat.**

Interesting information. Could have mentioned that before submitting the general application for everything on my plate.

I'm laughing now.

I'm going to start carrying this fucking pepper-flake thing in my pocket when I eat.

Portable anti-begging technology.

Dog approaches the bread.

Hand moves toward pocket.

Dog remembers another appointment.

In my head, I am now a man whose entire security policy is:

> Have some flakes, motherfucker.

The packet is doing all the work. She's already gone.

## The Aftermath

Later, she's curled up on the couch.

Eyes closed. Head on the cushion.

Absolutely exhausted.

Meat. Treats. More treats. Soup meat.

And then the unbearable discovery that one item in the house wasn't worth begging for.

Poor Bonnie.

Somehow she survives.
