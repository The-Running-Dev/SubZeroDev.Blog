This was Syracuse, probably twenty years ago. I'm playing basketball in the yard with one of my brother's friends, and the guy is taller than me.

Four, five, six inches. I don't fucking know the exact measurement. I'm about five-eight; he was probably six-two. Enough of a difference that you didn't need to get a tape measure out to establish who had the height advantage.

He thought he was hot shit.

<!-- truncate -->

I wouldn't have told you I was good at basketball. It was one of my better sports, sure. More to the point, I fucking loved it. I liked playing. And every so often, something ridiculous happened.

This was one of those times.

He goes for a shot.

I get up there and put my hand right on the fucking ball as he's about to shoot it.

Blocked.

The guy towers over me by several inches, and there I am, stopping the ball before he gets the shot away.

That's the whole moment. I remember that fucking block. I remember the height difference. I remember being right there when he went to shoot.

And I still find it funny.

You can look at two people standing beside each other and make a perfectly sensible prediction about which one is going to reach higher. Then they start playing, and sometimes the shorter one gets a hand on the ball anyway.

I don't need that moment to prove I was secretly an incredible basketball player. It was a block in a yard in Syracuse. One of those bits of playing that stays with you for years because it was fucking satisfying.

The guy was taller. He went for his shot. My hand was on the ball.

Twenty years or so later, that's still the part I can see.

I loved basketball enough to keep playing it, and sometimes, while I was playing, I got to do shit like that.
