# The Ridgeworm Chronicles

I live with a Rhodesian Ridgeback.

She's enormous.

Sometimes she folds herself on the rug in a position that makes her look less like a dog and more like a giant brown worm with optional legs.

I call this configuration the **Ridgeworm**.

Unfortunately, the Ridgeworm has other operating modes.

<!-- truncate -->

## The Outdoor Bathroom Incident

Bonnie somehow figured out how to get into the outdoor bathroom.

By the time I discovered this, the bathroom mat no longer qualified as a mat.

Wet wipes were fucking everywhere.

A proper little crime scene.

She saw me noticing the aftermath and ran off as if I were about to be furious.

I looked at the remains.

It was a garbage mat anyway.

"Glad you enjoyed yourself."

And that was pretty much the end of the investigation.

No courtroom. No trial.

The victim was a shitty bathroom mat.

## The Giant Worm

Later she was lying on the rug, folded into that ridiculous posture.

Big torso. Head tucked. Limbs apparently stored for future use.

A gargoyle rendered as a sausage.

Then she was facing me, and I climbed over her to kiss her.

She wriggled and play-bit at me.

For a moment I was blocking her route out, and she made her opinions known immediately.

The quiet Ridgeworm had become a mass of paws and teeth.

The amusing part to me was the speed of the transformation: floor ornament, then instant wrestling match.

Bonnie has no problem letting me know when she wants to move.

## Luke and Leia

This reminded me of two dogs from years ago.

My ex-wife had Luke and Leia, a pair of mystery mixes that looked something like Rottweiler/Labrador combinations.

Luke and I were best buddies.

I used to horse around with those dogs, rolling them over and climbing into their space, playing at being the big dominant idiot in the room.

Apparently some elements of my dog-interaction software are very old.

Bonnie is a larger, more muscular piece of hardware with her own distinct responses.

Which might explain why a quiet session of kisses can become a small wrestling match in approximately half a second.

## Meanwhile, Uncle Ben

This was the same stretch of time in which I was telling stories about multiple dead vehicles, international breakdowns, and how absurd my life is.

One minute: abandoned Audi in France.

Next minute: BMW engine.

Then: camper van, Lucifer suit, CB radio, e-bike.

Then I go home and the dog has turned the bathroom into a field of wet-wipe confetti.

People ask how I deal with all this.

What do you mean?

The dog tore up a garbage mat.

I cleaned up.

She looked like a worm.

I kissed the worm.

The worm objected.

**Apparently this is my life.**
