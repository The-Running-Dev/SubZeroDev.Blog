# There's Nowhere to Arrive

I was talking about the possibility of dying alone, and somehow peace started turning into another fucking destination.

A place I was supposed to reach. A better version of myself waiting at the end of all this work, presumably holding a certificate.

No.

<!-- truncate -->

There's nowhere to arrive.

But before I get to that, there is the less elegant part. The part where I look at my family, feel sorry for my mother, and recognize that the ending I imagine for her might be my ending too.

Alone.

That possibility doesn't come wrapped in philosophical wisdom. It comes with questions. Who the fuck gives a shit about me? Why should they? Do I need somebody to give a shit?

Those are uncomfortable questions. I asked them anyway.

I wanted things from my mother. I wanted things from my brothers. I wanted to be treated differently. I am not going to rewrite that into some heroic story about a man who never needed anybody and emerged from the womb emotionally self-sufficient.

What a magnificent load of bullshit that would be.

I cared. I wanted something from those relationships. Otherwise there would have been considerably less to talk about.

But how long does my peace have to wait for somebody else to become the person I wanted them to be?

What happens if they never do?

Am I supposed to keep my own life in a holding pattern until the family produces an acceptable conclusion? We could be here a while. The committee has already demonstrated some difficulties with the agenda.

I don't need anybody to validate my peace.

That is what I was trying to say. Whatever I wanted from them, however I wanted to be treated, my life is happening now. Their decisions are theirs. I still have to live with mine.

And when I go, I go.

I can die alone. So fucking what?

There is defiance in that sentence. There is uncertainty too. I don't know how my life ends. Imagining an empty room is not the same as having seen the future, and asking who cares about me doesn't prove that nobody does.

But I am trying to make room for the possibility without requiring someone else to promise me it won't happen.

I said I was working on it.

That was where the conversation started trying to help me toward the finish line. You don't have to have it all figured out. You can still be working on peace without pretending you've arrived.

And I had to stop that thought right there.

Arrived where?

We are so fucking good at doing this. Take a living experience and give it an entrance requirement. Take a thought about peace and turn it into a development plan. Now there is a successful future person, and the poor bastard alive today has another standard to fail.

Even acceptance gets a performance review.

I don't have a final enlightened version of myself to announce. I don't have a speech about how the pain was necessary or how grateful I am that everything happened exactly as it did. I haven't discovered a way to make every feeling pleasant.

I'm here.

I can feel sorry for somebody. I can remember what I wanted. I can swear about it. I can have a thought today that feels less settled tomorrow. None of that requires me to stand outside my life until I have learned how to live it properly.

There's no place to get to.

It's just life.

I'm here for now until I'm not.
