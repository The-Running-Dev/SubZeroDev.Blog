# The Ridgeback Infrastructure Upgrade

My dog had never chewed an electrical cord.

Not once.

Two years.

Then apparently one evening she looked at the shop vac sitting outside and decided:

**This cable has had a good run.**

<!-- truncate -->

I was gone for maybe two and a half hours.

The shop vac wasn't plugged in. The cable was connected to an extension cord, because the factory cable was only about four meters long and therefore functionally useless for anything involving an actual yard.

At some point, the Ridgeback dragged the cable halfway into the garden and chewed straight through it.

Not the hose.

I had specifically picked up the plastic hose because I thought:

> She's going to fuck with that.

No.

The hose survived.

The electrical cable was executed.

I found the damage later and pointed at it.

She ran.

Immediately.

People can debate endlessly about what dogs understand.

I don't particularly care.

She knows the difference between:

**WE ARE PLAYING**

and

**YOU HAVE FUCKED WITH MY SHIT.**

This was very clearly the second category.

So I was pissed.

I was going to make her sleep outside.

This was hardly Siberian exile. She has a crate, blankets, shelter, the entire fucking arrangement.

Then she started whining.

Eventually I let her back in.

She slept on the floor.

Justice had been served.

Except sometime during the night I woke up and discovered that the convicted cable murderer had quietly climbed into my bed and positioned herself directly next to my head.

I woke her up.

"Motherfucker."

Kicked her off the bed.

Apparently as far as she was concerned, the case had already been resolved.

---

The next day I started repairing the shop vac.

Which began with discovering that whoever assembled the thing had used some mystery fucking screw recessed halfway into the plastic.

Torx.

Fine.

I own enough tools to invade a small country, so eventually I found the right bit and opened it.

Then I realized something.

The original setup was already stupid.

The shop vac had a four-meter cable.

Then I attached another twenty-something-meter extension cord.

The connection constantly came apart while I dragged the vacuum around the property.

So I had zip-tied the two cables together.

It worked.

In the same sense that tying your bumper on with rope works.

Since I had already taken the machine apart because the Ridgeback had initiated an unscheduled maintenance event, I went to the electrical hardware store.

I came back with **34 meters of weatherproof cable**.

Then spent an unreasonable amount of time installing connectors on both ends because apparently attaching a fucking plug is now precision engineering.

But eventually:

One cable.

Thirty-four meters.

No extension cord.

No connector dragging through the yard.

No zip ties.

No random disconnections.

The shop vac is now objectively better than it was before.

Which means the Ridgeback destroyed my property and somehow forced an infrastructure upgrade.

I did not request this project.

I did not schedule this project.

There was no ticket.

There was no sprint planning.

There was simply:

**DOG → CABLE → FUCK.**

And several hours later I had a superior electrical system.

The only remaining problem is that the new cable is weatherproof.

It is not, as far as I can tell, Ridgeback-proof.
