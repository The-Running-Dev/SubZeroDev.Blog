# Ben the Legend

In Barcelona I joined a motorcycle meetup group.

About twenty riders would get together on Saturdays and head into the mountains. It was fucking awesome.

Then we planned a group ride in Mallorca.

This is the story of how I acquired a nickname I didn't ask for.

<!-- truncate -->

## The Ferry

We gathered on Friday evening. The ferry departed around seven at night, and we reached Mallorca roughly twelve hours later, around seven in the morning.

Have you tried sleeping overnight in a ferry chair?

Good fucking luck.

I hadn't been riding the motorcycle regularly. Maintenance wasn't exactly current. The tires weren't in great shape, or at least I hadn't checked them properly.

So naturally, when we got to Palma, we went riding.

## The First Fucking Curve

The group heads out.

We reach one of the first curves.

It's a left.

Everybody else goes left.

My bike decides we're going **straight**.

Into the gravel.

The front digs in, the rear comes around, and the machine pirouettes through something like a 360. I'm scrambling to get the fuck away from the thing before it lands on me.

The bike goes down. Front-end damage, forks, tires, plastics, various mechanical heartbreak.

And my left knee gets properly fucked.

I phone the guys. They come back. They help me.

Of course adrenaline has me saying I'm fine.

I'm not particularly fine.

## Two Months

I couldn't walk properly for a couple of months afterward.

But that was eventually over, and as soon as I was walking again I was back with the motorcycle group.

There was one immediate difference.

I wasn't moving fast.

We'd ride through the mountains in a column, and I'd end up so far behind that the designated rider keeping everyone together had to keep waiting for me.

Then the column would stretch and separate and they'd have to wait again.

I wasn't trying to establish a record. I was just riding.

After one of those outings, the group leader called me:

**"Ben the Legend."**

I was like, "Oh. Okay."

## Where the Name Came From

It wasn't some boast I invented.

I crashed. I hurt myself badly enough to be unable to walk for months. Then, when I could, I came back, rode slowly, and kept the whole column waiting.

Somebody else decided that qualified for a nickname.

Fine by me.

That motorcycle still had years of international absurdity left in it. Mallorca wasn't even the final act.
