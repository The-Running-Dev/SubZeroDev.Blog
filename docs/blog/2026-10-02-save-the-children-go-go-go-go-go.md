Oh my God, will you stop saving the goddamn children?

I swear to Christ, “the children” might be the most useful excuse adults have ever invented for their own shithead behavior.

Not actual child abuse. Not starving children. Not children who genuinely need somebody to protect them.

I mean:

**THE CHILDREN WERE SCARED.**

Well, fuck.

Stop everything.

Deploy the National Guard.

<!-- truncate -->

My dog got loose once.

She wasn't wandering around because I don't contain her. She got loose **despite the fucking containment protocol**, because apparently Rhodesian Ridgebacks occasionally discover vulnerabilities in perimeter security.

I wasn't even there when it happened.

Some children apparently got scared.

Nobody got attacked. Nobody got bitten. Nothing actually fucking happened.

But somebody's friend decided this was sufficient justification to jump at me like he wanted to kick my ass.

Because, obviously, when children have been frightened by a dog, the appropriate response is for a grown man to threaten another grown man with violence.

That should calm everybody down.

**SAVE THE CHILDREN.**

Then today I'm walking home with the dog.

She's on a leash.

We're on a public street.

I have a plastic bag in my hand, so naturally I smack her with it because this is apparently how we entertain ourselves.

She starts jumping around.

Playing.

Being a dog.

I walk maybe fifty meters and hear a kid absolutely losing its fucking mind behind me.

Screaming. Crying.

I turn around and realize there's a school or playground behind a fence that I hadn't even noticed. There's a woman holding a baby or toddler or something, and she gives me this fucking dirty look.

Apparently my dog jumping around **on a leash, on a public street** has frightened a child I didn't know existed.

Okay.

The kid got scared.

Kids get scared.

That's a thing children do.

Nothing happened.

There wasn't even an incident.

But apparently somewhere in that interaction I was supposed to have detected the presence of an unseen child behind a fence and maintained an appropriate emotional exclusion zone around it.

Because:

**SAVE THE CHILDREN.**

What makes this particularly funny is how often the same adults operate under completely different rules when it's their behavior we're talking about.

Somebody drives like an asshole.

Blocks something.

Does some inconsiderate shit in public.

You say something.

And suddenly:

**WHO THE FUCK ARE YOU?**

Now they're angry.

Now they're yelling.

Now *you're* the asshole because you pointed out the thing they actually fucking did.

But their kid sees my leashed dog jumping around fifty meters away?

Well, apparently I have failed in my responsibilities to society.

This isn't even specifically a children thing.

It's the same fucking behavior everywhere.

There's a guy who lives a few houses away from me.

One day my dog was whining on my balcony.

Not in his yard.

Not outside his bedroom window.

On **my balcony**.

So this motherfucker drives over, stops outside my property, sits in the driver's seat, and starts honking.

Not “Hey.”

Not knock on the gate.

**HONK. HONK.**

Apparently I have been summoned.

Eventually I come outside and he starts bitching at me about the dog whining.

I just started laughing.

Because what the fuck else am I supposed to do with this?

You drove three houses down, parked outside my property, and honked until I came outside so you could formally complain that my dog was making dog noises on my balcony.

This is fucking hilarious.

He did not find it hilarious.

He wanted a different reaction.

He wanted me to take him seriously.

Instead:

“Get the fuck out of here, asshole.”

And I went back inside.

He drove away.

I've noticed this repeatedly.

People get verbally aggressive and somehow expect the aggression itself to create authority.

They get louder.

They get closer.

They puff themselves up.

My aunt does this too. She's about five feet tall and can become spectacularly verbally aggressive.

And I'm standing there thinking:

Buddy.

If I blow on you, you fall over.

But apparently we're doing **VOLUME = AUTHORITY**.

Unfortunately, I have exactly the wrong reaction to this.

I laugh.

Sometimes I'm laughing with people.

Sometimes I'm laughing at the situation.

Sometimes, yes, motherfucker, **I'm laughing at you.**

And apparently nothing makes an angry person angrier than discovering that their intimidation routine is fucking funny.

“WHY ARE YOU LAUGHING?”

Because it's funny, asshole.

Then they yell louder.

Which makes it funnier.

So I laugh harder.

Which makes them yell louder.

Which makes me laugh harder.

At some point I'm not even participating anymore.

They're running the entire escalation loop themselves.

Which reminds me of the Jack Russell up the street.

Every time I walk past with my Ridgeback, this little motherfucker completely loses his shit.

He sees her.

BARK.

Spin.

BARK.

Spin.

BARK.

Spin.

He's basically turned himself into a small canine centrifuge.

And I don't try to calm him down.

I'm not there to help him.

I'm there to make fun of him.

So every time he starts:

**“GO GO GO GO GO!”**

And the little bastard somehow discovers another two thousand RPM.

**BARK SPIN BARK SPIN BARK SPIN.**

Which, now that I think about it, is pretty much the same interaction.

Someone gets increasingly worked up.

They get louder.

They become more ridiculous.

I start laughing.

They become even more worked up because I'm laughing.

**GO GO GO GO GO!**

Maybe that's the real lesson here.

Actually, no.

Fuck lessons.

Sometimes the dog is just spinning.
