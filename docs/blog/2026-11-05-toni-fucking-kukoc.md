We got to Syracuse in May. School didn't start for us until September.

I was eighteen, and I had a very reasonable plan for the intervening months: play fucking basketball.

Nonstop.

The Vietnamese kids I played with were shorter than me. They were also better than me. Both things were true, and I had no trouble recognizing either of them.

Their nickname for me was Toni Kukoč.

<!-- truncate -->

Toni fucking Kukoč.

I never claimed to be some great basketball player. Basketball was one of my better sports, but mostly I just loved playing it. Give me a few months before school and people to play with, and that was plenty.

Why the nickname? I'm guessing there were a few reasons. Europe figured into it. I was taller than they were. And then there was this ridiculous fucking shot.

One of the kids would get right on my back, guarding me hard. I'd turn around, fade away, and put the ball in.

Every fucking time, as I remember it.

Leave me alone, though? Nobody guarding me? I'd miss.

Apparently the defender was an essential part of the equipment.

You'd think removing the person trying to stop the shot would improve my chances. That's a perfectly reasonable theory. My basketball didn't seem particularly interested in cooperating with it.

Put somebody on me. Have him really try to guard me. Turnaround, fadeaway, basket.

Give me the easy version, and suddenly we have a problem.

That's the sort of ridiculous shit I remember doing. I'd go toward the basket, do something that didn't look like it was going to work, and the fucking ball would go in. People would look at me like: what the fuck was that?

I didn't have to have a beautiful explanation for it. It went in. I was playing basketball. I was having a fucking good time.

And those kids were better than me. I keep saying that because there's no reason to improve the story by pretending otherwise. I was taller. They were better. I had this turnaround fadeaway that became dependable precisely when somebody was making it difficult.

That was enough to get me a nickname I still remember.

When I think about those months, I don't start calculating whether all that playing was productive enough. We arrived in May. School was in September. There was basketball in between, and I loved it.

I wasn't waiting to become good enough to enjoy myself. I was already out there, playing with people who were better than me, occasionally doing something ridiculous that worked.

And for that summer in Syracuse, apparently, I was Toni fucking Kukoč.
