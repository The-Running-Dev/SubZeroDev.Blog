I was decent at basketball when I was a teenager. Fourteen, fifteen, sixteen—I don't fucking know. Somewhere around there. What I remember much more clearly is that I loved playing.

My classmates in high school in Bulgaria didn't pick me to play with them. They were too fucking full of themselves. So I played with other kids.

<!-- truncate -->

That was it. I still got to play basketball.

I didn't need those particular people to pick me before I could enjoy the game. There were other kids, and I played with them. The thing I wanted to do was still there.

And I fucking loved it.

I didn't have to be good at it. I didn't have to become somebody through it. I wanted to play basketball. That was already a perfectly good reason to play basketball.

Somewhere along the way, through playing, I got good enough. But getting good wasn't the condition I had to meet before I was allowed to enjoy myself. I loved playing while I was getting there. I loved it when I made a shot. I loved it when I missed.

And sometimes I'd go to the basket and do some ridiculous shit.

A layup, a fadeaway, whatever the fuck that particular attempt turned into. The ball would go in, and people would look at me like they were trying to work out what they'd just seen.

What the fuck was that?

I don't need a beautiful explanation for every ridiculous shot. The fucking ball went in. I was playing basketball. I was enjoying myself.

And if it didn't go in? I still loved the game.

That's the part people seem to have trouble leaving alone. They want to work out how good you are. Where you fit. Who's better. Whether what you're doing counts for enough.

Meanwhile, I'm over here having a perfectly good fucking time.

This is an overall philosophy of my life. I don't have to be good at something. I don't have to be anybody in something. I don't have to find an end game for every single thing I enjoy doing.

Sometimes the whole reason to do the thing is that you fucking enjoy it.

Do it.

You can get better while you're doing it. You can have a good day or a bad one. You can make the shot or miss the shot. None of that means you have to hand the enjoyment over to somebody else and wait for them to approve it.

And then people try to rank themselves against me.

They're a better basketball player. A better drinker. Better at this, better at that, better at whatever the fuck they've decided we're comparing today.

Okay.

I'm not collecting drinking records, either. The fact that somebody can make a comparison doesn't make it a contest I need to enter.

You can challenge me. You can think you're better than me. You can be better than me.

I laugh because I don't know what you expect me to do with that information. Stop enjoying something? Recalculate whether all the time I spent having fun was worth it? Find out where I rank before I'm allowed to do it again?

I loved playing basketball when my classmates didn't pick me. I loved it when I got decent at it. I loved it when some ridiculous shot went in, and I loved the game when it didn't.

There is no fucking end game I have to reach to make that count.

Be better.

I don't care.
