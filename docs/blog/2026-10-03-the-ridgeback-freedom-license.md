# The Ridgeback Freedom License

The Ridgeback walks fine on leash most of the time.

Not perfect.

I still have to say:

> Here.

> Heel.

Then she comes back beside me.

For approximately as long as the environment remains insufficiently interesting.

Off leash is a different operating mode.

Off leash introduces **smells**.

<!-- truncate -->

## Freedom

I let her wander.

She is not trying to run away.

She is not disappearing over the horizon.

She is doing Ridgeback business.

Walk.

Sniff.

Look around.

Continue.

Then she finds a smell.

Not a normal smell.

A **Smell**.

Capital S.

Face goes down.

Brain disconnects.

The dog is no longer receiving external messages.

I call her.

Nothing.

Again.

Nothing.

She has apparently opened a direct socket to the molecular history of Bulgaria and does not intend to close it until the entire dataset has been downloaded.

Fine.

I walk over.

Leash goes back on.

> You don't listen?

> Now you get no freedom, dumbass.

License revoked.

## Conditional Access

That is really what off-leash freedom is.

Conditional access.

You can wander because you have demonstrated that when I need you back, you come back.

The entire system depends on recall.

Ignore recall and the system collapses.

Not emotionally.

Not as punishment.

Operationally.

If I cannot reliably interrupt whatever the dog is doing, then she does not get to be operating independently next to roads, animals, people, and whatever other ridiculous shit exists in the village.

So the sequence is simple:

Freedom granted.

Dog discovers irresistible smell.

Recall ignored.

Freedom revoked.

Leash restored.

Ticket closed.

## Except Cats

Here is the part that makes the whole thing funny.

Somehow I have taught this animal not to chase cats.

A cat appears.

The Ridgeback sees it.

You can watch the thought forming.

Head changes angle.

Body notices.

Predator software begins loading.

> I could absolutely—

I say:

> Uh-uh.

> No.

And she stops.

Not after chasing it for fifty meters.

Not after I yell six times.

She stops **mid-thought**.

You can practically see the process terminate.

> ...fair enough.

And we continue walking.

This enormous dog can look directly at a moving cat, experience the ancestral call of several thousand years of canine nonsense, and somehow exercise restraint.

## But the Smell Has Root Access

Then we reach a patch of grass.

Interesting molecule detected.

Immediate total system compromise.

Cat:

Policy understood.

Smell:

**Root access.**

That is the part I cannot stop laughing about.

The cat is objectively more exciting.

It moves.

It runs.

It is visibly another animal.

The dog has every imaginable reason to chase it.

No.

Rule installed.

Works.

But apparently some invisible piss-mail left on a shrub by a village mutt three hours earlier can defeat the entire command hierarchy.

The Ridgeback becomes a forensic investigator.

Nothing else exists.

## Heel Is Still Beta

Heel works too.

Mostly.

She knows what it means.

She comes beside me.

Walks correctly.

Then something happens.

Dog behind fence.

Plastic bag.

Smell.

Person.

Leaf behaving suspiciously.

The world contains too many interrupts.

So I repeat:

> Heel.

She returns.

Then five minutes later:

> Heel.

Again.

There is no mystery here.

The dog is young.

The environment is interesting.

The command exists.

The implementation remains under active development.

## License Revoked

And that is how the morning went.

Started off leash.

Everything fine.

Then somebody found a smell worthy of religious devotion.

Recall temporarily ceased to exist.

So now:

Leash.

No wandering.

No appeal.

No committee meeting.

No lengthy explanation of why the policy exists.

The Ridgeback does not need a PowerPoint presentation.

She needs to walk.

Tomorrow she may get freedom again.

Or later.

Whenever.

The license is renewable.

But it is conditional.

Because apparently this animal is capable of suppressing the urge to chase a cat while simultaneously being defeated by grass.

Cats:

**Controlled.**

Heel:

**Mostly operational.**

Recall:

**Environment-dependent.**

Smells:

**Unpatched zero-day.**

Ridgeback Freedom License:

**Temporarily suspended.**
