# A Lot of Moving Parts

There's a lot happening at once.

<!-- truncate -->

I want to build my own file manager. I need to inspect the existing tools properly, including Warp, and work out what I want to make. There's MemoryKit and the GitHub setup repository, and the rest of the software work.

I also want to get set up with InterNations so I can go out and meet interesting people.

The BMW needs an engine. My landlord offered me a cheap car, which raised another possibility: buy a whole car for roughly what I might pay for an engine. Except I already have a car with problems, and I don't know whether I want to buy a different collection of problems.

Meanwhile I decided to clear the kitchen cupboards, so the physical environment now looks worse in the middle of trying to improve it.

A lot is uncertain. That's what I'm pointing out.

I got lost in it last week. Now I'm okay. I got up. I'm doing things. Some of this old bullshit is genuinely amusing me as I talk about it.

I don't want the number of open questions mistaken for a statement that I'm currently falling apart. I can have several unresolved decisions, a dismantled kitchen and a list of things to build, and still be feeling pretty good.

The kitchen is a mess. The engine decision is unresolved. The projects are in progress. Those are the conditions of the day. Today I'm up and involved in them.

## Maybe I Will Bottle Some Cocktails

Another idea occurred to me: could I bottle cocktails in Bulgaria?

White Russians. Bloody Marys. Long Islands. Drinks I know and like.

People at bars have told me I surprise them with my cocktails. My answer was that I've been a fucking alcoholic for thirty years. A fairly dark answer to a compliment, but it is what I said.

I'm not convinced there is a business here. My immediate idea is smaller: buy some bottles, make a few batches and offer samples to local bars to see what they think.

I've concerns about what running a business here could involve: regulations, corruption, protection demands and all the rest. Those are concerns I bring to the idea, not things I've established about this particular experiment.

I might make some for myself and see what happens with the samples. I doubt it will catch on, but it made me curious.

I don't know yet what it would take to make and sell these properly. I'm curious whether the cocktails people enjoy when I make them would interest anyone in bottles.

It can remain a question for now. I've enough other things in motion without pretending this has already become another company.
