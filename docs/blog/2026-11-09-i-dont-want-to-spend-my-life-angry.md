I don't want to be angry at the world. Unfortunately, the world keeps submitting material, and Discount Store Marcus Aurelius appears to have subscribed to the full package.

<!-- truncate -->

Politicians on television. Family. People speaking with absolute confidence about things they've never experienced. A conversation in which I'm trying to say one thing and somebody helpfully argues with something else.

The emperor has objections.

Many objections.

At some point I would like to stop chairing the fucking objections committee.

## I Know the Philosophy

There is a particular embarrassment in having an entire clearance-aisle Stoic persona and still getting pissed off at the television.

I know the jurisdiction discussion. I know Mike Johnson does not report to me. I have no administrative authority over the confidence of strangers in Texas, the opinions of people in Bulgaria, or the human species' approach to thinking.

The paperwork is clear.

And there I am, annoyed anyway.

Discount Marcus would like to return the philosophy. It appears to have arrived without the button that makes you permanently serene.

The man on the box looks very calm.

The man using the product would like a fucking word.

Knowing something intellectually does not mean I have stopped feeling everything that makes it difficult. I can tell you I don't control people and still wish they would listen. I can understand that an argument is going nowhere and still be standing in it, explaining the same thing again.

No emperor's robe required.

Just the ordinary human talent for continuing to touch the thing that irritates you.

## Please Argue With My Actual Sentence

During this conversation, I said America has social programmes.

This developed into a clarification that I hadn't said America was a socialist country.

An excellent discussion. Unfortunately, I had not ordered it.

I was talking about political scare rhetoric. Suddenly we were inspecting tax rates and explaining distinctions I wasn't asking to have explained.

I had asked for a historical correction earlier, and I got one. I accepted it. Marx's timeline was something I hadn't known.

That didn't mean every sentence afterwards was an application for a compulsory audit.

Sometimes I want somebody to hear the thing I actually said.

A modest imperial request.

Apparently it requires several departments.

## The Joke Gets Tired Too

I can make this funny.

A disappointed emperor in Bulgaria, pushing a shopping cart full of slightly damaged philosophy, objecting to the world while fully aware the world has not appointed him its supervisor.

There is plenty to work with.

The cart is ridiculous. The emperor is ridiculous. The fact that I can recognize the absurdity while still being fucking annoyed is ridiculous.

But underneath that, I said something plain.

I'm tired.

I'm annoyed.

I feel misunderstood.

Those aren't punchlines, even if I can put a punchline next to them.

I don't want to spend whatever time I have left repeatedly furious at humanity. I don't want every confident idiot to become another item I carry around. And I don't know what to do with the anger anymore.

The comic persona does not have a better answer prepared.

He has a cart.

We may have overestimated his qualifications.

## It Comes and Goes

I was asked whether the anger passes or stays with me.

It comes and goes.

That is what I know.

I am not angry every moment. I am also not going to pretend it has stopped bothering me because I can explain it or make it sound entertaining.

There isn't a revelation waiting at the bottom of this page. I haven't discovered a new breathing technique, forgiven the entire planet, or become a marble statue with excellent emotional regulation.

Nor am I ending this with an inspirational declaration that tomorrow I will push the cart with renewed purpose.

Maybe tomorrow I will have something else to talk about.

Right now, I am tired of being angry and tired of being misunderstood.

Discount Marcus is still capable of finding the situation absurd.

He would also appreciate some fucking peace.

The cart has no comment.
