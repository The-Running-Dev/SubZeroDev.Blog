I was sitting at the bar drinking Scotch and smoking cigars.

Young Buck was on my right.

His father was on my left.

Tinder was apparently feeling adventurous.

A guy appeared.

I looked at Young Buck.

"This one's for you."

And that's where he made a tactical error.

<!-- truncate -->

He looked at my phone and immediately noticed something.

"Ohhh. You have men and women selected."

Apparently this was supposed to be embarrassing.

Now, I don't remember selecting that.

It is entirely possible that I did.

Maybe I clicked something at some point.

Maybe Tinder decided I needed broader horizons.

I have no fucking idea.

But none of that mattered.

Because I understood the joke.

The joke was supposed to be me suddenly explaining myself.

"No, no, no, I'm not gay."

"I must have changed the setting accidentally."

"Tinder fucked something up."

Some frantic little performance establishing that the appearance of a man on my screen was definitely, absolutely, unquestionably not something I wanted anybody to misunderstand.

Nah.

I looked at the guy.

Good-looking dude.

So I swiped right.

Right there.

In front of Young Buck.

## Match

Tinder immediately produced the match.

Well.

That improved things considerably.

I turned the phone around and showed him.

There.

He likes me too.

What do you have to say now?

Young Buck had absolutely no fucking idea what to do with this information.

Which was the entire point.

The attempted joke required my cooperation.

It required me to accept that there was something embarrassing about the possibility.

It required me to defend myself.

Instead I matched the motherfucker.

## Now What?

There is a particular kind of teasing that only works if the person being teased agrees with the premise.

You're supposed to flinch.

You're supposed to deny.

You're supposed to demonstrate that the thing being implied about you would be unacceptable.

If you don't?

The machinery stops.

This is especially funny in Bulgaria, where casual homophobia is hardly difficult to encounter.

A man being interested in another man can still be treated as inherently funny or shameful.

Fine.

I'm not playing.

You want to tease me because Tinder showed me a dude?

I'll match him.

Oh look.

It's mutual.

Now what?

There wasn't a speech.

There wasn't some grand statement about sexuality.

I wasn't trying to educate anybody.

I was sitting at a bar drinking Scotch and smoking a cigar.

Young Buck tried to make fun of me.

I saw an opportunity.

And sometimes the funniest response to somebody trying to embarrass you is simply to refuse to understand why you're supposed to be embarrassed.

**So fucking what?**
