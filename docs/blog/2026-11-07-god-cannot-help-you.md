It was Saturday night. I had nothing else to do. A stranger on Telegram had apparently lost $300,000 and wished to discuss my financial misfortunes. By the end of the evening, I had referred the matter to my various friends in the underworld and the intelligence agencies, with Satan available to receive the visitor. A perfectly ordinary allocation of resources.

*The Lucifer Chronicles — Another George*

The profile said Opal. The introduction said Jo Anne.

We had barely begun, and the cast already required clarification.

She had, she explained, come across a list containing the names and phone numbers of people affected by online scams. She herself had lost about $300,000. The experience had been devastating. Had I ever lost money to a scam?

“Oh yes.”

I had met this production before. [George Myers](https://blog.subzerodev.com/this-conversation-never-happened/) had arrived with a similar tragedy and eventually attempted to transfer the proceedings to a WhatsApp recovery group. That encounter had concluded with an improvised mafia subplot and the disappearance of the conversation.

Here was another George. Different name. Larger loss. The familiar invitation to become fellow sufferers.

Game on, motherfucker.

Jo Anne asked how much I had lost.

“Enough to want to murder someone.”

This was the point at which an ordinary conversation might have developed a question or two.

Jo Anne supplied sympathy. She understood why I was angry. Losing hard-earned money could leave a person devastated. I had every right to feel upset.

Then she asked whether I had contacted the FBI.

The presentation remained admirably committed to its itinerary.

“I don't need the FBI to solve my problems. I got my ways.”

My ways.

A remarkably economical phrase. No names, no telephone directory, no tedious burden of establishing that I knew anybody at all. Just the suggestion of arrangements.

In the commentary accompanying my little Saturday-night performance, I was considering government connections. CIA. Mossad. Some suitably ridiculous intelligence apparatus. I had no friends in those agencies or in the underworld. I was inventing the supporting cast as I went.

Things do not have to be real for someone to believe them. Jo Anne had presented $300,000 without producing the money. I felt entitled to introduce a few people without producing the people.

Then I thought of *Burn Notice*.

“My name is Michael Weston, and I used to be a spy.”

That was the introduction I was considering sending. A television opening, repurposed as a deeply questionable Telegram credential. The evening had not been issued a script, but I was willing to borrow one.

Michael remained available while Jo Anne developed her evidence.

She had found my number on the list, she said. Other people's numbers too. Even her own number was there. She would send it so I could look.

“Sure, why not.”

The exhibit arrived.

Green text on black. Processes. Tokens. Privilege escalation. Extraction. A table with names and amounts concealed behind asterisks and six visible phone numbers.

It possessed all the visual confidence of a computer doing something extremely important in a film whose technical adviser had left before lunch.

“Take a look at the green codes.”

The green codes.

Well, I must be fucking blind. I could see the colour. I could see the theatrical enthusiasm. I was still awaiting the part that established anything about me.

“Do you know who you are talking to right now?”

Jo Anne replied that we were strangers getting to know one another. She understood my caution. Hopefully, we could have an honest conversation.

An honest conversation. Between Opal, Jo Anne, and a man contemplating his entrance as Michael Weston.

I let that sit for a while.

Later, the subject became suspicious messages from strangers.

“Oh you mean like the unsolicited message you sent me?”

She acknowledged that her own opening had been unexpected too, then explained that she had become cautious about strangers online.

A delicate professional difficulty, given her current method of meeting people.

I asked what she wanted from me.

Signal, apparently. She felt more comfortable there, particularly with all the suspicious messages she was receiving on Telegram. Would I download it so we could continue?

I already had Signal. I was not giving this person another place to contact me. There was no installation problem to solve.

Jo Anne said she wanted nothing from me. She was a victim herself. We could share information, support one another, and find a safe, legitimate way to recover what we had lost.

Then came the app-store link and instructions to let her know once I had installed it.

George had required WhatsApp. This production preferred Signal. Financial resurrection continued to involve relocating the audience.

By then, I had enough material. Michael Weston could stand down. I wrote my own closing statement.

Here it is in full, because the cactus and the intelligence agencies belong in the same record:

> Alright, enough, I know your scam, you are at least the 5th person trying to do this here. Blow me, suck a bag of dicks and sit on a cactus. There is a special place in hell for asshats like you. Satan is waiting with open arms and you will get what is coming to you. And now that I have your info, I will backchannel all of it to my various friends in the underworld and the intelligence agencies. Good luck to you, god cannot help you.

“My various friends.”

Such a comfortably established social network. Criminal connections. Intelligence contacts. All apparently reachable by backchannel, all introduced with the administrative ease of forwarding a receipt.

I had begun the paragraph with a cactus and ended it having accounted for earthly and celestial jurisdiction.

Mossad and the CIA had been warming up in my commentary. The final message gave the intelligence agencies a collective booking. I trusted my various friends to recognise themselves.

Satan, however, received an explicit appointment.

Then that final courtesy:

“Good luck to you, god cannot help you.”

I wished her luck. I immediately clarified the limits of the available assistance. Heaven had no role in this matter; Hell was expecting her with open arms.

It was fucking absurd. That was the fun of it. A stranger had brought me a recovery pitch, and I had furnished an international intelligence subplot with an afterlife attached.

I blocked the account and deleted the conversation.

The intelligence community could take the evening off. Satan, I had already specified, was expecting company.

Good luck to you.

**God cannot help you.**

*Lucifer, signing off.*
