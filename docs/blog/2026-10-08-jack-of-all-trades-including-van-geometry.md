# Jack of All Trades, Including Van Geometry

There is a phrase I've heard my entire life:

> Jack of all trades, master of none.

Which always sounded vaguely insulting.

Then somebody inevitably adds the rest:

> But oftentimes better than a master of one.

<!-- truncate -->

That version makes considerably more sense to me.

Because at some point I seem to have accumulated a ridiculous number of completely unrelated abilities.

Not necessarily mastery.

Just enough competence in enough weird areas that occasionally somebody looks at me and asks:

> How the fuck do you know how to do that?

Good question.

No idea.

Apparently one of those skills is now **van geometry**.

The more I drive Van Diesel, the more accurately I know where every corner of the thing is.

I can tell where the right tire is without seeing it.

I can put it between potholes.

I can park the van about ten centimeters from a fence and get out completely unconcerned while everyone else is wondering whether I'm about to remove part of the property.

This joins an increasingly stupid collection of things stored somewhere in my head under:

> Might be useful later.

Computers.

Electrical shit.

Mechanical shit.

Construction shit.

Cooking.

Driving.

Networks.

Random undocumented APIs.

And now:

**Exact spatial location of the right tire of a twenty-five-year-old Mercedes van.**

I wouldn't claim to be a master of most of these things.

That's probably the point.

The useful part isn't being the greatest person alive at one narrow thing.

It's having enough accumulated competence that when life throws some bizarre new problem at you, there's a decent chance something in the pile applies.

Jack of all trades.

Master of none.

But apparently pretty fucking good at parking.
