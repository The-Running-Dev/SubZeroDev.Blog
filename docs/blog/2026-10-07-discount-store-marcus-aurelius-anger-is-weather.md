# Discount Store Marcus Aurelius: Anger Is Weather

A Serbian woman told me about an argument with her therapist.

Her therapist told her she should find her peace.

She thought people should be more angry.

The world has gone mad.

Humanity is sedated.

People are not angry enough.

And I am sitting there thinking:

Sure.

Maybe.

Also, so fucking what?

<!-- truncate -->

This was not me trying to prove her wrong.

That was the funny part.

I was not trying to have a point.

I was trying to explain a worldview.

She could be right that people should be angrier.

Her therapist could be right that she should find peace.

Both things can fit inside the same universe without reality throwing an exception.

## Peace Is Not Approval

Somewhere along the way people started treating peace as if it means approval.

If I am peaceful about something, apparently I have accepted it morally.

If I stop being angry, apparently I have surrendered.

If I am not actively furious, apparently I do not care enough.

I do not buy any of that.

I have peace.

Sometimes I am also fucking angry.

Those are not opposites.

I can look at something and say:

> This is fucked.

Then I can do whatever I can actually do about it.

And then I can continue living without requiring the entire world to rearrange itself before I am allowed to sleep.

That is not surrender.

That is refusing to make my nervous system collateral damage.

## I Still Fight Battles

This was the thing I realized while talking to her.

I did not stop fighting battles.

Jesus Christ, have you met me?

I still argue.

I still call bullshit.

I still get pissed off.

I still fight internet battles that nobody asked for.

I still push against things I think are wrong.

What changed is that at some point I stopped making **myself** the battlefield.

That part is harder to explain because I do not remember a ceremony.

There was no moment where Marcus Aurelius descended from the heavens and handed me a coupon for inner peace.

I used to be angry at the world.

Angry at my parents.

Angry at life.

Angry that things were not what I thought they should be.

Then somehow I got here.

I have no fucking idea exactly how.

Maybe I let go.

Maybe I had always been doing things for myself and eventually the internal fight became unnecessary.

Maybe enough shit happened that demanding reality be different started looking like an expensive hobby.

I do not know.

But I know the difference.

## Anger Can Be Useful

I am not anti-anger.

That would be ridiculous.

Anger can move people.

Anger can tell you a boundary has been crossed.

Anger can get you off your ass.

Anger can make you say:

> No. Fuck this.

Sometimes that is exactly what is needed.

Sometimes anger is also just burning CPU while nothing useful happens.

Same emotion.

Different context.

That is why asking whether anger is "right" is often less interesting to me than asking:

> What is the anger doing?

Is it moving something?

Protecting something?

Pointing at something?

Or is it just making you miserable while the thing you are angry about continues not giving a fuck?

## Discount Store Philosophy

At some point she told me certain things are simply right or wrong.

Sure.

Certain things.

Then she told me life is not exactly black and white.

Also sure.

That is basically where I live now.

Not in some magical land where nothing matters.

Not in nihilism.

Not in cynicism.

Just in the irritating middle where context keeps showing up and ruining all the clean slogans.

Peace can coexist with anger.

Acceptance can coexist with disagreement.

Caring can coexist with letting go.

Disappointment can coexist with continuing.

I can strongly prefer that reality behave differently while also understanding that my preference does not create jurisdiction.

Apparently this makes me a philosopher now.

Fantastic.

No university.

No robes.

No marble bust.

Just some asshole in Bulgaria telling a Serbian woman over chat:

> You can be angry and still have peace.

Discount Store Marcus Aurelius has reopened the clearance aisle.

The cart still clacks.

Humanity is still humanity.

I am still capable of getting furious.

And underneath it, somehow, there is peace.

Anger is weather.

It can storm.

The house does not have to become the storm.
