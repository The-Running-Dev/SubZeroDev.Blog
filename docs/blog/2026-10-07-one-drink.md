# One Drink

This may be the least dramatic blog post I have ever written.

I bought booze.

I bought meat and cheese.

I expected to drink.

I had one drink.

I ate.

I watched an absolutely terrible Jason Statham movie.

Then I went to bed.

That is it.

And for me, that is fucking monumental.

<!-- truncate -->

I have never been good at "one drink."

That phrase has historically sounded more like a theoretical unit of measurement than an actual stopping point.

One drink is what happens before the rest of the drinks.

So when I woke up the next morning and thought about the night, the interesting part was not that I had successfully controlled myself.

I did not.

Not in the dramatic sense.

There was no internal negotiation.

No white-knuckle argument.

No heroic:

> You can do this, Ben. Put down the glass.

I just had one.

Then apparently I was done.

## No Fight

That is what caught my attention.

No fight.

I bought the alcohol expecting the evening would probably become an evening of drinking.

The infrastructure was available.

Gin existed.

Food existed.

I was at home.

Nothing prevented a second drink.

I simply did not have one.

Then I went to sleep.

For somebody else this may be so ordinary that writing about it looks ridiculous.

Fine.

For me it was unusual enough that I noticed.

## The Rest of the Night Was Still Absurd

Naturally the surrounding evening was not normal.

I had spent hours talking philosophy with a Serbian woman.

Anger.

Peace.

Parents.

The world.

Whether humanity is sedated.

What sits under anger.

Whether peace means surrender.

Then she had what she called a "date."

Some guy was coming over.

She was bored.

She was not sure whether she would have sex.

This uncertainty had, on previous occasions, shown a remarkably consistent ability to resolve itself.

I told her:

> Well, you won't be bored shortly.

Then she disappeared from the conversation.

Scientific inquiry remains inconclusive.

Meanwhile I watched Jason Statham participate in whatever cinematic crime had been committed against the audience.

One drink.

Food.

Bed.

## I Do Not Know What It Means

This is where people usually want the lesson.

What changed?

Why?

Is this a new pattern?

Does it mean something?

I have no fucking idea.

It happened once.

That is the fact.

I am not turning one data point into a religion.

Maybe I was tired.

Maybe the food changed the rhythm.

Maybe the conversation occupied me.

Maybe the movie was so bad my liver filed a protest.

Maybe none of that matters.

I just know I did something I do not normally do.

## Sometimes the Interesting Thing Is the Absence

A lot of my stories are about action.

Build the thing.

Fix the thing.

Argue with the thing.

Drive the thing.

Break the thing.

Repair the thing.

Fight humanity in the comments.

This one is interesting because the event is mostly an absence.

The second drink did not happen.

Neither did the third.

There was no battle about it.

Nothing dramatic replaced them.

I ate.

I watched a shitty movie.

I slept.

Maybe that is why it feels significant.

The system did not crash.

The system did not require intervention.

The process just stopped.

One drink.

Apparently that is an option.
