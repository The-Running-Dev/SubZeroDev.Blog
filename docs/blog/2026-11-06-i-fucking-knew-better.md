I was looking back at the messages I sent Yav, my younger brother. The reply about Star Wars. The last message. All of it. And I felt absolutely fucking stupid for having said any of it.

<!-- truncate -->

The last message thanked him for reading what I had written. Told him he could include Martin if he wished. Explained that I wanted him to hear my account and understand why I had objected to what he wrote. I wasn't asking for a point-by-point response. I didn't want to keep debating the past.

Then I said I was still open to reconnecting in the present, with some disagreements left unresolved. That reaching out had been about that.

There it was. Sent.

I had help making the wording more tame. More acceptable. And looking at it now, that wasn't how I fucking felt.

But even beyond the wording, what the fuck was the point of any of it? Why reach out? Why say anything?

I've seen over and over how little these people give a shit about me. This isn't information I only just acquired. I've had a lifetime of trying to explain myself, trying to say this or that, trying harder, thinking maybe it would make a goddamn difference.

It hasn't.

And I'm still fucking doing it.

Of course I wanted them to hear me. Of course I wanted to make a difference. I know that. Telling me I wanted my brothers to care doesn't answer the question. So fucking what? Wanting it doesn't explain why I tried again with everything I already knew.

That's what makes the decision feel so stupid. I knew the history. I had seen it repeatedly. And there I was, sending another message.

When I said this, ChatGPT kept telling me it hurt. I had to correct that too.

It doesn't hurt. It feels absolutely stupid.

I am looking at something I chose to do and wondering why the fuck I did it. Every fucking time, I end up asking myself that same question.

And of all the things I hate feeling, I hate feeling stupid.
