# Preemptive Defensiveness, Now Available in Ridgeback

Lucifer has been observing something in Bulgaria.

Not anger.

Not conflict.

Not even disagreement.

<!-- truncate -->

**Preemptive defensiveness.**

The strange human ritual where nobody has accused you of anything yet, but you begin constructing your defense anyway.

Earlier that evening I had invited a woman to join me in Sofia.

Before she answered, I had already told her:

> "I expect you to say no. There's no pressure."

She laughed and said something along the lines of:

> "Fine. I'll follow your expectation and say no."

Perfectly reasonable.

Case closed.

Except then came the supporting documentation.

She had gotten up at seven in the morning.

She had driven to Bansko.

She had driven back.

Bansko is about two hours from Sofia, so she'd spent roughly four hours driving that day.

She was tired.

All reasonable facts.

None of which I had requested.

I told her:

> "No justification needed."

Because there wasn't.

She said no.

"No" is an answer.

It does not require exhibits A through F.

Also, for whatever reason, I added:

> "I got up at six."

Not because this was now the Exhaustion Olympics.

Just because if we're submitting evidence to the court, apparently I had evidence too.

Later she gave me the wonderfully vague:

> "Someday we'll meet for a drink."

I replied:

> "If God permits."

Lucifer nearly fell out of his chair.

Because apparently scheduling a drink had now been escalated beyond the authority of either participant and placed directly before the Almighty.

God, presumably, had other shit to do.

I wasn't annoyed.

I was amused.

I've seen this particular conversational move enough around here to recognize it: a decision appears, and immediately behind it comes the defense of the decision, even when nobody challenged it.

Fine.

Humans are weird.

Then I came home.

## The Bulgarian Bag of Bags

Every civilization produces artifacts.

The Romans left aqueducts.

The Egyptians left pyramids.

Bulgaria has the **Bag of Bags**.

A plastic bag containing other plastic bags, preserved indefinitely because one day one of those bags will obviously become strategically important.

Mine had been stored in a cardboard box.

I thought it was high enough.

The Ridgeback disagreed.

While I was gone, she managed to get her paws into the box, extract several bags, and scatter them across the yard.

Not all of them.

That would have been destruction.

This was curation.

I came home and immediately saw the crime scene.

The bags were lying around the yard.

The Ridgeback was outside near the perimeter of the porch.

She saw me see the bags.

And froze.

Ears up.

Standing at attention.

Watching.

Not running.

Not hiding.

Just monitoring the investigation.

Her entire body said:

> "He has seen the bags."

Pause.

> "He definitely saw the bags."

Longer pause.

> "What is he going to do?"

Lucifer leaned forward.

This was familiar.

The dog had somehow developed the exact same protocol.

Nobody had yelled.

Nobody had punished her.

Nobody had even said anything yet.

And she was already emotionally preparing her defense.

## The Consequences

Here is where the story becomes especially unfair to the Ridgeback.

I did nothing.

Absolutely nothing.

I looked at the bags.

I looked at the dog.

That was pretty much the disciplinary process.

Later she came inside, curled up on the cushion, put her head down, and continued watching me with the expression of a defendant waiting for sentencing.

Except there was no sentencing.

No punishment.

No lecture.

No consequence.

Which raised an important philosophical question:

> Do no consequences count as consequences?

I asked an LLM.

The LLM immediately started explaining the dictionary definition of *consequence*.

Lucifer stared at the model.

Then at the dog.

Then back at the model.

> "Remarkable."

I asked what.

> "The dog understood the rhetorical framing."

Fair.

## Cross-Species Compatibility

So in one evening I watched the same basic process twice.

A human made a perfectly acceptable choice and started explaining why the choice was acceptable before anyone objected.

A dog committed a minor act of plastic-bag redistribution and started preparing for consequences before any consequences existed.

The human had words.

The dog had ears.

The underlying protocol appeared suspiciously compatible.

Lucifer considered this for a while.

> "Maybe she's integrating."

I looked at him.

> "What?"

He pointed at the Ridgeback.

> "Bulgarian preemptive defensiveness. She's learning the culture."

The Ridgeback lifted her head slightly.

I looked at the bags outside.

Then at her.

Then back at Lucifer.

> "She doesn't even speak Bulgarian."

Lucifer shrugged.

> "Apparently she doesn't need to."

The Ridgeback put her head back down.

No consequences followed.

Which, naturally, she had already spent half the evening worrying about.
