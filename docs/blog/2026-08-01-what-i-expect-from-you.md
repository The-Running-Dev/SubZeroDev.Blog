# What I Expect From You  
*(The Mirror)*

> This page is not demanding.  
> It’s symmetrical.

I don’t ask for anything I don’t give.

<!-- truncate -->

---

## I Expect Honesty

Say what you mean.
Mean what you say.
If something isn’t working, say so.

I don’t punish clarity.
I rely on it.

---

## I Expect Curiosity

You don’t have to know everything.
You do have to care.

Ask questions.
Challenge assumptions.
Stay interested longer than is comfortable.

That’s where the good stuff is.

---

## I Expect Ownership

If you take something on:
- see it through,
- surface problems early,
- don’t outsource responsibility.

Mistakes are fine.
Avoidance is not.

---

## I Expect Adult Communication

I don’t do:
- passive aggression,
- politics,
- or indirect signaling.

If there’s tension, we talk.
If there’s disagreement, we explore it.
If there’s a decision, we make one.

---

## I Expect Mutual Respect

Not hierarchy.
Not deference.
Not performative politeness.

Respect looks like:
- listening,
- not interrupting,
- and assuming competence by default.

---

## I Expect Space to Think

I don’t operate well in environments where:
- urgency is constant,
- interruptions are the norm,
- and reflection is treated as laziness.

If everything is always on fire, something upstream is broken.

---

## I Expect You to Say “No” Sometimes

Blind agreement is not collaboration.
Pushback is healthy.
Boundaries matter.

If you never disagree with me, something is wrong.

---

## What I Don’t Expect

I don’t expect:
- perfection,
- certainty,
- or endless availability.

I don’t expect you to:
- impress me,
- keep up appearances,
- or be anything other than yourself.

---

## What This Page Is Really About

This is not about standards.
It’s about **fit**.

If reading this felt:
- reasonable,
- grounding,
- or reassuring,

we’ll probably work well together.

If it felt:
- heavy,
- demanding,
- or uncomfortable,

that’s useful information too.

---

> Alignment beats talent.  
> Every time.

> This page exists so we don’t have to learn that the hard way.
