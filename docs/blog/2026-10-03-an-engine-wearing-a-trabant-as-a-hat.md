# An Engine Wearing a Trabant as a Hat

I dropped the camper at the shop and there it was.

A Trabant.

Bright paint.

Tiny body.

Four wheels that looked like somebody had attached them to a garden appliance.

I stopped.

> Holy shit.

I do not know how people fit inside these things.

It is a fucking lawnmower on wheels.

And naturally, within about thirty seconds, my brain had arrived at the only reasonable question:

**What if you put a completely inappropriate engine in it?**

<!-- truncate -->

## The Trabant Problem

I do not want a Trabant because it is a good car.

That would be a very strange argument.

I want one because it is ridiculous.

It looks like something designed by a committee whose requirements were:

- must technically transport humans;
- must use approximately no materials;
- must fit somewhere between a refrigerator and a wheelbarrow;
- must continue existing long after anyone expected it to.

And somehow the little bastards are still around.

That alone is charming.

But charm is not enough.

The obvious problem is insufficient violence.

## Victor

This idea is not new.

Years ago, around college, there was this guy Victor.

Vietnamese kid.

A couple of years ahead of me, I think.

The details are fuzzy because this was a lifetime ago, but one story never left my brain.

Somebody had taken an old Volkswagen Beetle and done something deeply unreasonable to it.

Gutted the back.

Sacrificed the rear seat.

Put some enormous engine where passengers were previously expected to exist.

I do not remember what the engine actually was.

In my memory it has become Ferrari-sized and offensive.

That may not be historically accurate.

The important part was the engineering philosophy:

> Where does the giant engine go?

**Back seat.**

> But people sit there.

**Not anymore.**

I remember hearing that story and thinking:

> Holy crap. I want to do that.

Apparently the thought survived.

## Passenger Capacity: Two

So now I am looking at this Trabant.

Little communist lawnmower.

And the old Victor story boots back up.

Remove the back seat.

Engine bay?

Who gives a shit about the engine bay.

The engine lives wherever the engine physically fits.

Put something absurd in the middle or the back.

Build the rest of the car around the mistake.

Passenger capacity:

**Two.**

Engine capacity:

**Unreasonable.**

This would not be a tasteful restoration.

I have no interest in tasteful.

Tasteful is what you do when you respect the original design intent.

This idea begins by rejecting the original design intent as insufficiently funny.

## The Entire Joke

Imagine driving down the road at normal Trabant speed.

Nothing dramatic.

Fifty kilometers an hour.

Maybe less.

You are just peacefully operating what appears to be a brightly colored piece of lawn equipment.

Somebody catches up behind you in a modern car.

They see the Trabant.

They laugh.

Of course they laugh.

Look at the fucking thing.

Then the road opens.

You put your foot down.

And somewhere behind the front seats, an engine that has absolutely no business being inside this vehicle wakes up.

**WAAAAAAAAAAAAA.**

The Trabant disappears.

Not accelerates.

Not pulls away.

**Leaves the timeline.**

And the person behind you just sits there.

Looking through the windshield.

Trying to reconcile the evidence.

> Did I just get passed by a Trabant?

Yes.

Yes you did.

## Performance Art

That is the appeal.

Not speed by itself.

Fast cars exist.

You can buy fast cars.

I already understand fast cars.

The funny part is the contradiction.

The shell says:

> Eastern European lawnmower.

The engine says:

> Terrible decisions have been made.

The driver behind you expects one reality and receives another.

For five seconds, physics becomes a practical joke.

That is not transportation.

That is performance art.

At some point the project would stop being a Trabant with an engine swap.

The suspension would need changing.

The brakes.

The drivetrain.

The cooling.

The chassis.

Probably half the fucking car.

Eventually very little of the original engineering would remain except the body and the joke.

Which is fine.

Because the body is the joke.

The final object would not really be a Trabant anymore.

It would be something much better:

**An engine wearing a Trabant as a hat.**
