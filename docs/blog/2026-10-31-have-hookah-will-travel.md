I own a portable fucking hookah lounge.

<!-- truncate -->

It fits in a little hard case. Pack everything up, carry it somewhere, unpack it, and now that place has a hookah lounge. Very straightforward. The world provides somewhere to sit; I provide the equipment.

Dobry has experienced this business model firsthand.

We went to a park right in the center of Sofia. I had the case with me, because apparently going to a park requires luggage when you’re me. I filled the hookah from a fountain, set everything up, lit it, and there we were: sitting in a public park, bullshitting and smoking hookah.

A perfectly pleasant arrangement.

Dobry was embarrassed.

“This is embarrassing.”

“What the fuck is embarrassing?”

We’re sitting here. We’re talking. We’re smoking hookah. Everything appears to be functioning as intended. I’ve successfully transported a leisure activity from one location to another. Personally, I feel the operation deserves some credit.

People walked past and looked at me like I was a fucking alien.

Then they went about their business.

That was the entire incident. A man with a hookah had briefly entered their field of vision. They processed this information and continued with their day.

Meanwhile, Dobry had to sit beside the alien.

I understand that carrying a little hard case into central Sofia, filling a hookah from a fountain, and establishing a smoking lounge in a park might look ridiculous. I was there. I watched myself do it.

It was also a lovely way to spend some time.

***

In the village where I am now, I took the concept on tour.

One night I thought, fuck it, I’m bringing the hookah to the bar.

Packed it up. Carried it over. Asked if I could set it up in the outside lounge.

“Yeah, no problem.”

Excellent. Planning permission secured.

I set it up, lit the coals, and sat there drinking and smoking my hookah. When I was finished at that bar, I packed everything back into the case.

Then I went to another bar.

And did exactly the same fucking thing.

There’s something about that sequence that amuses me enormously. Finish your drink. Pack your lounge. Relocate the establishment. Resume operations.

Most portable things are designed to make travel easier. Mine allows me to bring more sitting down.

I had become a hookah bar that visited other bars.

***

I know I’m a ridiculous motherfucker.

Knowing it is part of the enjoyment. Every so often I tell one of these stories and hear myself properly. Here’s a grown man explaining, quite reasonably, how he filled his hookah from a fountain in Sofia while his embarrassed friend sat beside him. Here he is again, carrying his smoking equipment between village bars like a very small touring production.

And I think: yes. That sounds like me.

It amuses me to no end.

There’s a particular pleasure in remembering your own life and finding that you’ve repeatedly behaved like a character somebody would be accused of overwriting.

Surely he doesn’t take the hookah to the next bar.

Of course he fucking does. He’s already got the case.

***

Eventually, whenever I get VanDiesel fixed, or otherwise get myself traveling again, I want to take this operation to a beach.

Bring the hookahs. Find somewhere to sit. Put up a sign:

**FREE HOOKAH. COME SMOKE WITH ME.**

That’s the whole invitation.

Come sit down. Have a smoke. Bullshit for a while.

I like the simplicity of it. I already own the ridiculous equipment. I already carry it places. A sign is hardly a major expansion.

For now, it’s an idea. The beach is still ahead of me, along with getting moving again. But I can picture that sign very clearly.

***

When I remember these stories, I feel like a fucking legend in the most absurd possible sense.

A jester. A clown. Some ridiculous caricature of a human being wandering through life with his props packed neatly in a hard case.

I’ve managed to become a recurring character in my own stories, and apparently one of his defining traits is that he can establish a hookah lounge wherever somebody lets him sit down.

A park in Sofia. One village bar. Then another.

Hopefully, eventually, a beach.

I’m curious where else this idiot is going to turn up.

He seems to have everything packed.
