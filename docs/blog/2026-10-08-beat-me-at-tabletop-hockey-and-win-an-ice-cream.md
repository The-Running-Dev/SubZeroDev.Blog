I had some weird fucking dreams.

Something about my brother.

Couldn't tell you what actually happened in them. The dream evaporated as soon as I woke up, like dreams tend to do when they decide they've delivered whatever message the subconscious allegedly had in mind.

But I did wake up with an idea.

I have a giant tabletop hockey game sitting in the back of my camper.

And I love that fucking thing.

<!-- truncate -->

I'm also really good at it.

Now, that last statement is obviously dangerous. There is no more efficient way to summon an eight-year-old who will humiliate you than to announce that you're really good at a game.

Nevertheless.

I don't want to get rid of the hockey table. I don't want it collecting dust in Van Diesel for the next decade, either.

So I thought: why not take it to the bar?

## Welcome to the Championship

Here's the pitch.

Find a time when there are kids around, set up the game with the bar's permission, and offer a challenge.

**Beat me and you get free ice cream.**

That's it.

First to five? First to ten? We'll figure out the rules.

I'm paying for the ice cream.

The kid gets a shot at glory.

I get to play a game I love.

And the bar gets to host what I am now choosing to regard as the least prestigious sporting championship in Eastern Europe.

Everybody benefits.

Unless I'm not nearly as good as I remember.

In which case everybody benefits even more, because the ice-cream expenditure may become a significant operating cost.

## Adult Division

Naturally, the adults get a different prize.

Beat me, get a drink.

No, I haven't designed a full international governing body for this yet.

We don't need a board of directors.

We need two little plastic hockey teams, a scoreboard, and people willing to challenge the idiot who brought sports equipment to a bar.

The important part is that the prize must be tempting enough to make somebody think:

*How hard can it be?*

And that's when they discover I've spent years preparing for this completely meaningless confrontation.

Or they beat me immediately.

Also possible.

Highly inconvenient to my legend.

## The Final Boss Has Entered the Bar

The image I keep coming back to is some kid staring at a giant hockey table, thinking there's an easy ice cream available.

Then he gets absolutely demolished.

Then his friend tries.

Then somebody's father steps up because surely a grown man can handle this.

And suddenly an entire afternoon has been consumed by a man defending an imaginary title he invented at breakfast.

I cannot emphasize enough that **none of this has happened yet**.

It's a plan.

I haven't taken the table out of the camper. I haven't made arrangements with the bar. I haven't challenged a single child.

For all I know, the first kid will wipe the floor with me.

And if that happens, I'll buy the ice cream.

Because the whole point is to turn a thing I love, currently sitting in storage, into something people can enjoy.

Also, I would like to be undefeated.

Both can be true.

**Come for the ice cream. Stay for the completely unnecessary athletic rivalry.**
