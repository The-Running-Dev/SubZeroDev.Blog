# There Is Nothing to Gain From This Behavior, But It Will Confuse the Cunty

On the ride home, I passed a sign for the little village where Cunty McCunterson lives.

And for one moment I had a completely ridiculous thought.

What if I just rode over there?

Not to do anything.

Not to talk to anybody.

Not to accomplish anything.

Just ride past the house, turn around, and come back.

There is absolutely nothing to gain from this behavior.

But it would confuse the Cunty.

That sentence made me laugh.

Which is also why I did not do it.

<!-- truncate -->

## The Return Drive-By

The whole joke was the symmetry.

I have watched people perform these little drive-bys and presence checks and indirect maneuvers.

So when I saw the village sign, my brain generated the parody immediately.

Fine.

Let's reproduce the behavior.

Twenty-kilometer unnecessary side quest.

Ride past the house.

Be seen.

Leave.

Maybe somebody reports the sighting.

Maybe somebody starts wondering what I was doing there.

Maybe I happen to see her and say:

> "Hey. My mother wants to talk to you. Call her."

Then ride away.

Perfectly stupid.

Which is exactly why it works as a joke and not as a plan.

To mock ridiculous behavior by physically reproducing the ridiculous behavior would require me to spend my own afternoon participating in it.

No.

The thought was enough.

## The Better Version

The genuinely funny version would have required nothing from me.

I was already riding through the area.

Suppose she had happened to drive past me.

That's it.

I would be there for a completely ordinary reason.

Van Diesel was at the mechanic.

I had the e-bike.

I had found a good route home.

No operation.

No scheme.

No message.

Just some asshole on a fat-tire bicycle doing 45 kilometers an hour.

But if somebody already believes everything has a hidden purpose, then an unexplained sighting becomes raw material.

Why is he here?

Where is he going?

Was he in the village?

Where is the camper?

What is he doing?

I do not have to manufacture the story.

People are perfectly capable of doing that themselves.

Often they write something far more elaborate than anything I could have invented for them.

## I Usually Say Nothing

This is one of those things people sometimes misunderstand about me.

I do explain what I am doing when there is a reason to explain it.

But when somebody has already built their own story about my motives, I usually do not spend much energy trying to correct the screenplay.

I stay quiet.

That silence is apparently very productive.

People fill it.

They take whatever evidence they think they have, run it through whatever model they have built, and produce a conclusion.

Then sometimes reality does something else.

And they are surprised.

Not because I sat down and designed some brilliant counter-move.

Usually I did not plan any of it.

I just did whatever I was going to do.

## You Think You Know the Pattern

That is the part that has always amused me.

People decide they have figured me out.

They think they know what I will do next.

They build a model.

Ben does X.

Therefore Ben will do Y.

Therefore we can predict Z.

Okay.

Maybe.

Then I do something else.

Not to break the model.

Not to prove anything.

Not because I give a shit about the prediction.

I am usually not even thinking about their model.

I am just not operating from the script they invented.

That has happened to me before.

Somebody builds a pattern from a handful of observations and then treats the pattern as if I signed a contract with it.

I did not.

## The Gate

The same thing applies to the property bullshit.

I have been accommodating.

Show up while I am here.

Fine.

Show up unannounced.

Also fine.

I will open the gate.

My problem is not that somebody might arrive.

My problem is specifically the sneaking-around-while-I-am-not-there bullshit.

Because then I get to spend my time wondering:

What did they do?

What did they move?

What did they take?

Did somebody come in?

I am tired of thinking about it.

So the answer eventually became embarrassingly simple.

Chain the gate when I leave.

Visible camera pointed at it.

The camera does not even have to work to communicate the point.

If I am home, come by.

If I am not home, the gate is closed.

Want access?

Call me.

This is not some elaborate psychological operation.

It is a chain.

The reason is for me.

I do not want to keep carrying the question around in my head.

Whatever effect that has on anybody else's predictions is their business.

## Whatever Story You Like

Maybe one day somebody decides they have determined I am not home.

Maybe they show up.

Maybe I am there.

That would be funny.

Not because I engineered it.

Because their prediction would simply collide with reality.

And I would be standing there thinking:

Hello.

No reveal.

No speech.

No explanation of how their intelligence operation failed.

Nothing.

People can make whatever predictions they like.

They can invent whatever motives they like.

They can decide that a bicycle on a road means something.

They can decide a camper missing from the yard means something.

They can decide silence means something.

I do not have to participate in the story.

That is the useful part.

I do what I am doing.

They do whatever they are doing.

Whatever effect it has, it has.

And occasionally, entirely by accident:

**It will confuse the Cunty.**
