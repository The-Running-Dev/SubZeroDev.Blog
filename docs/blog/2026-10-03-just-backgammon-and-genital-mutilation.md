I thought I was fucking unhinged.

I had accepted that. Made room for it. Put a chair out. Lucifer lives here; occasionally he opens a dating app.

Then I encountered a profile that made me sit up and think: *Jesus Christ, lady.*

<!-- truncate -->

The profile said she was looking for new friends.

Lovely. Friends. A perfectly reasonable thing to want. Perhaps some conversation. Perhaps a shared interest. Perhaps we could all briefly remember how to interact with another human being.

Then came the introduction:

> I'm not doing sex, if you try something, i will rip your dick
>
> Just backgammon and arcade 🙂🙃

Well.

That escalated before we'd even selected a fucking board game.

I have no objection to someone looking for friends. I have no objection to someone saying they don't want sex. Excellent. Understood. We have established what we're doing.

But apparently we also needed to establish what could be detached.

And then, immediately, backgammon.

That is the part I keep coming back to. The backgammon.

You cannot just drop a threat of genital mutilation into your introduction and then slide a cheerful little board game across the table like the previous sentence was about parking.

*Anyway. Do you know how to play?*

I used to think the most dangerous part of backgammon was discovering that someone had learned a different set of rules. Apparently there is a whole other level of house rules I was unaware of.

## The Terms of Friendship

Let me make sure I've understood the invitation.

Sex: no.

Backgammon: yes.

Arcade: yes.

Continued attachment of all existing body parts: subject to terms and conditions.

And two cheerful emojis to round it off.

🙂🙃

One upright. One upside down. I don't know whether those are mood indicators or the before-and-after diagram.

I'm sitting there reading this, and somehow **I** have become the person concerned about the tone of the conversation.

Me.

Lucifer.

The man whose immediate spoken reaction included enough fucks to require their own luggage.

And yet the reply I typed was:

> Would you now? That's rather violent. Get some help.

That's what I typed. And I meant it.

Read it again. *Would you now?*

Who the fuck am I? A retired headmaster? A Victorian gentleman inspecting an unusually disturbing calling card?

There should be a monocle involved. I should be standing beside a fireplace, holding this dating profile between two fingers.

*Madam, your proposed leisure activities appear to include a regrettable incident.*

## I Thought I Was the Wildcard

That was my actual surprise.

I thought I was the fucking wildcard here.

I can be loud. I can be profane. I can open my mouth and cause the surrounding conversation to require a small administrative pause.

But I opened a dating app and found myself responding to somebody's introduction with restrained concern.

There was no argument. No rejected advance. No conversation in which someone had ignored a boundary.

This was the fucking brochure.

*Welcome. We have backgammon.*

*Please familiarise yourself with the dismemberment policy.*

I don't know whether she intended it as a joke. All I have is the profile. But whatever the intended delivery, those sentences were sitting next to each other, in that order, presenting themselves as an invitation to friendship.

And I was sitting on the other side of the screen, apparently auditioning for the role of the sensible adult.

Jesus Christ.

I thought I was fucking unhinged.

Then somebody made backgammon sound like a fucking hostage negotiation.
