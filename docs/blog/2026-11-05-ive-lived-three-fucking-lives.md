Some online test gave me an IQ of about 140. Maybe 142.

I've never had it officially measured. I don't buy the number as some established fact about myself, and I don't particularly care whether somebody else does.

According to some people, 142 puts you near genius territory.

Okay. Now what the fuck am I supposed to do with that?

<!-- truncate -->

None of it is official. And even if it were, who gives a shit?

I know I'm intelligent. I believe my emotional intelligence is high too. I don't need to pretend I think I'm stupid just because I don't want to turn a test result into an identity.

But what really matters to me, when I try to describe who I am, is lived experience.

The amount of fucking shit. The nonsense. The situations. The horseshit I've been through.

What number are we using for that?

That's why I find these comparisons absurd. Somebody wants a figure that can be put beside somebody else's figure, and suddenly we're supposed to know something important about the two people.

I can tell you the number an online test spat out. I can also tell you I don't trust it enough to claim it as a fact. Neither statement begins to describe what it has felt like to live my life.

I don't look back at everything and think, yes, that was a 142 experience. What the fuck would that even mean?

I've lived.

And I've lived, and I've lived.

If somebody asked me to describe my life, the sentence I'd reach for would be: I've lived three fucking lives.

I'm around fifty, and that's what it feels like. Three fucking lives inside the one I've actually had.

I don't mean three qualifications. I'm not submitting a score for the amount of life I've accumulated. I'm trying to describe the sheer amount of it. How much there has been to go through, to deal with, to remember.

An IQ figure doesn't carry that. Neither does saying my EQ is high. Those may be ways people discuss abilities, but they're not the words I reach for when I think about all the living.

And if somebody doubts the test result, fine. It was an online test. Doubt away. Even an official result wouldn't go back through my life and change what happened in it.

The number isn't what I would tell you about first.

I'd tell you I've lived three fucking lives.

That's the description that actually feels like mine.
