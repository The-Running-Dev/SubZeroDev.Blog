# Apparently This Is My Life

After spending hours reconstructing thirty years of vehicle history, I noticed something.

The cars are not the story.

They're just one particular filing cabinet full of ridiculous shit that happened to me.

And apparently the new material arrives daily.

<!-- truncate -->

## Van Diesel

Here we are in 2026.

The motorcycle I've owned since 2007 is dead. The BMW engine melted after a glorious encounter with my own driving. Another BMW engine appeared out of nowhere.

And I now drive a Mercedes camper van named **Van Diesel**.

Sometimes I drive it in a Lucifer suit.

I have a CB radio like a trucker. There's an electric bicycle in the back. If I decide to disappear to Greece or Spain for the winter, that's at least a plausible idea. Internet? Starlink is the plan.

After all the engines, ferries, tow trucks, and customs officers, this is apparently where the automotive storyline has arrived.

## The Woman at the Auto Service

I was getting Van Diesel sorted at an auto service and got talking with a woman who'd independently discovered the camper-plus-e-bike arrangement.

Her reason for getting the electric bicycle was that the nearest groceries were about seven kilometers away.

Reasonable.

Meanwhile I'd been riding more than twenty kilometers each way on mine to collect the camper from the shop.

After I paid the bill she actually came back outside to inspect my e-bike.

Battery life? Is mine fancier? Does pedaling put charge back into the battery?

I didn't know about that last one. Said I'd check.

Naturally I forgot.

Later, after riding between places and turning the bike off and back on, I saw that the battery gauge had gone up. Whether it's energy recovery or the gauge adjusting after the load, the bike was still giving me material to investigate.

She asked what I did.

"IT."

Also, I told her, I hadn't worked in IT for three years.

She could make her own conclusions.

"What are you going to do for internet?"

"Starlink."

This is apparently a perfectly normal conversation in my life.

## Meanwhile, at the Hookah Lounge

At some point I was recounting these stories while sitting on the toilet at the hookah lounge.

And somehow I pissed all over myself.

Which is a rather aggressive editorial interruption.

So I cleaned up the mess I'd made.

And continued.

The universe does not seem interested in allowing me to finish a philosophical observation without providing an immediate practical demonstration.

## The Ridgeback

Then I got home and discovered Bonnie, my enormous Rhodesian Ridgeback, had found her way into the outdoor bathroom.

A shitty bathroom mat had been disassembled.

Wet wipes were everywhere.

She saw me discover the scene and ran off like I was about to be furious.

But it was a garbage mat anyway.

"Glad you enjoyed yourself."

Later she folded herself into an enormous brown worm on the rug. I went over to kiss her and she immediately had strong opinions, expressed with teeth and paws.

One day it's a vehicle, another day it's a dog, another day it's me somehow needing to clean up my own piss.

## Apparently

Somebody asked me recently how I got through one of my breakdown stories.

What do you mean, got through?

The intercooler fell off. The police stopped me. The tire blew. Somebody helped. I got where I was going.

The story is absurd when recounted. While it was happening, it was just the thing happening.

Then another thing happened.

I'm not sitting around inventing a comic-book character named Uncle Ben.

Sometimes I'm sitting in a camper with a CB radio, wearing Lucifer's clothes, wondering where to drive next.

And sometimes I'm sitting on a toilet, telling the story, and making another fucking story.

**Apparently this is my life.**
