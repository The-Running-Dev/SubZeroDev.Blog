# Ten Whole Centimeters

I was watching some video criticizing Gen X.

At some point they said Gen X isn't particularly *aware*.

Whatever the fuck that means.

My immediate thought was:

> Have you seen Uncle Ben drive?

<!-- truncate -->

Because apparently we're discussing awareness as some grand psychological concept while I'm driving Van Diesel down Bulgarian roads thinking:

> That pothole goes left of the right tire.  
> That one goes right of it.  
> There. Perfect.

The more I drive that ridiculous moving apartment, the more precisely I know where every part of it is.

Especially the right tire.

I don't need to see it.

I just know.

Two potholes coming up?

No problem.

Thread the fucking needle.

And people have been surprised by this before. Not specifically the pothole thing, but how quickly I seem to know the dimensions of whatever I'm driving.

Van Diesel is enormous compared to a normal car.

Yet I can back it up next to the fence until someone watching starts getting visibly nervous.

Then I stop.

About ten centimeters away.

They look at the van.

They look at the fence.

They look at me.

Meanwhile I'm thinking:

> What?

> There are still **ten whole centimeters**.

Apparently this counts as spatial awareness.

I just thought it was parking.
