The kid remembered me.

I did not fucking remember him. At all.

That's a useful detail to establish before this becomes a story about two old friends catching up. I didn't know his name when we played basketball, and when we met again about a year later, I didn't know who the fuck he was.

<!-- truncate -->

He asks if I remember him.

No. Who the fuck are you?

We played basketball together, he tells me.

Oh, shit. Now I remember you.

The game comes back. He's about twenty-five, I'm around fifty, and we're playing in the August heat. He's good. He's got energy. He's also got the considerable advantage of being roughly half my fucking age.

I'm thinking I'm too old to be playing that kind of ball in that kind of heat. I love basketball, but loving basketball does not make the age difference or the temperature disappear.

And he thinks he's hot shit.

Sometimes that gets me in a mood.

I don't generally set out to prove something to people. I go to play. But every so often, somebody makes me feel like showing him that the older guy can still do a few things.

So I block the shit out of him.

He tries to get something going under the basket, and I'm all over him. Like a fly on shit. All that youth, all that energy, and somehow Uncle Ben is still there.

I remember that. Once he reminds me which game we're talking about, I remember blocking him. I remember him being good. I remember playing.

What I hadn't carried around for the intervening year was his fucking face.

And now he calls me Uncle Ben.

He came up with that himself. Every time he says it, I chuckle. I'm not entirely sure how much of a friendship we've got, but the nickname is fucking hilarious to me.

There's the age difference, sitting right there in the name. Uncle Ben. The older guy. And there's also the fact that the older guy apparently did enough on the basketball court to remain recognizable a year later.

Meanwhile, I needed the reminder.

That's the part I keep coming back to. He remembers this guy who blocked him. I remember having a basketball game once somebody points me back toward it. We were in the same game, but we clearly weren't carrying the same souvenir.

I never went there to create a lasting impression. I went there because I fucking love playing basketball. Sometimes I play well. Sometimes I get in a mood and stay on somebody half my age until he discovers the afternoon isn't going quite how he expected.

Then life moves on.

Until, a year later, somebody recognizes me and I have to ask who the fuck he is.

Now I'm Uncle Ben.

I'll take it. It makes me laugh every fucking time.
