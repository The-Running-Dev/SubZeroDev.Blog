# Castlevania, But It's My Fucking Life

I looked in the mirror today.

The hair had opinions.

Not normal hair opinions.

Anime-villain opinions.

The kind of hair that says:

> I have already calculated your defeat.

Excellent.

Apparently this is how an animated series starts.

<!-- truncate -->

I joked that maybe I should make one.

Then I remembered *Milo Murphy's Law*.

The Weird Al one.

The entire premise is basically that ordinary life around one person produces cascading catastrophe and he has adapted to it so thoroughly that it is just Tuesday.

That felt uncomfortably familiar.

Then another reference clicked.

*Castlevania.*

Not the plot.

Not vampires.

The **vibe**.

Dark adult animation.

Dramatic lighting.

Serious framing.

Everybody looks like they are carrying the burden of history.

Now imagine using that visual language for my actual fucking life.

## The Shop Vac

Open on a ruined village compound at sunset.

Wind through the trees.

Clouds moving slowly.

Lucifer stands alone in the yard.

Long coat.

Anime-villain hair.

A thirty-four-meter electrical cable lies coiled around his feet like an ancient serpent.

The Ridgeback appears in silhouette.

Music rises.

Lucifer looks toward the gate.

Then down at the cable.

Long pause.

> "Who the fuck knows what a meter is?"

Cut to black.

Tell me that isn't an episode.

## The Beautiful Part Is That Nothing Needs Inventing

This is what made the idea suddenly more interesting.

I do not need to write an animated series.

I have already written it.

The blog is full of episodes.

Broken cars.

Village NPCs.

Dogs doing dog shit.

Dating disasters.

Cocktail lounges.

Philosophy appearing in places where philosophy has absolutely no business being.

Random conversations with old men.

Family feuds.

Computers.

Cables.

Doctors.

Bureaucracy.

Things I tried to repair becoming infrastructure.

The source material exists.

All I would be doing is changing the rendering engine.

## Lucifer

Lucifer is already the obvious protagonist.

Not Satan.

Not theology.

The blog version.

The narrator who looks at ordinary human nonsense with the exhausted expression of somebody who has been assigned to this server and cannot get reassigned.

He doesn't need superpowers.

His power is apparently:

> observing bullshit in real time.

Put him in the village.

Give him the hair.

Give him the black clothes.

Give him a laptop.

Give him a screwdriver.

Give him thirty-four meters of cable he absolutely did not understand before purchasing.

Now we have character design.

## The Ridgeback

The Ridgeback requires almost no adaptation.

If anything, animation may have to tone her down to remain believable.

She sleeps like a gargoyle.

Appears silently behind food.

Destroys things for reasons known only to her.

Turns ordinary tasks into combat encounters.

Looks majestic for twelve seconds.

Then steps directly on the thing I have spent ten minutes carefully folding.

Perfect secondary character.

No dialogue needed.

Just eyes.

Paws.

Chaos.

## Random NPCs

This may be my favorite part.

The world does not need a giant cast bible.

It needs recurring NPC classes.

**Village Old Man.**

Appears beside gate.

Has opinion.

May own restored Soviet vehicle.

**Cocktail Lounge Regular.**

Has been quietly tracking my dating activity without authorization.

**Bartender.**

Has seen enough.

**Random Tinder Human.**

Requests deep conversation.

Discovers excavation equipment.

**Family Antagonist.**

Arrives occasionally to advance property subplot.

**Mechanic.**

Receives vehicle.

Vehicle develops entirely new issue before reaching mechanic.

**Doctor.**

Attempts medicine.

Receives Ben.

Good luck.

This thing writes itself because life apparently keeps spawning procedural encounters.

## Prestige Animation for Stupid Problems

The contrast is the joke.

Do not animate it like a conventional comedy.

No bouncy sitcom energy.

No giant reaction faces.

No cartoon boing noises.

Treat every stupid event like the fall of Constantinople.

The shop vac button doesn't work?

Close-up.

Lucifer's eyes narrow.

Cut to internal mechanism.

Orchestral sting.

He whispers:

> "The external button is no longer connected to the internal button."

The Ridgeback watches from the doorway.

Thunder.

Somewhere in the distance, the clothes rack falls over again.

No one reacts.

## Then I Realized the Blog Is Already Storyboard Material

Take one post.

Find one absurd beat.

Strip everything else away.

Forty-five seconds.

Sixty seconds.

Maybe ninety if the thing earns it.

Done.

YouTube Short.

The blog becomes the long-form source.

The animation becomes the compressed version.

One does not replace the other.

The blog says:

> Here is the whole ridiculous chain of events.

The short says:

> Look at this one fucking thing.

That is a very different production problem from:

> I should make an animated series.

An animated series sounds enormous.

A sixty-second scene sounds like Tuesday.

## One Episode at a Time

Episode:

**Apparently I Bought Infrastructure**

Lucifer repairs shop vac cable.

Unrolls it.

Keeps walking.

Keeps walking.

Camera pulls back.

Still walking.

Reaches gate.

Looks at cable.

Looks at house.

Looks at gate.

> "Oh."

Beat.

> "Apparently I bought infrastructure."

End.

Another:

**What Are You, a Turtle?**

Old man says he doesn't like being on his back.

Lucifer slowly turns.

> "What are you, a turtle?"

Old man's wife continues drinking.

End.

Another:

**The Ridgeback Has Chosen Violence**

No explanation required.

## Is This Actually Good?

No idea.

This is the part where AI is basically useless as a judge.

Ask an AI whether your creative idea is good and there is a significant chance it will enthusiastically explain why you are a visionary.

Thank you, machine.

Very useful.

Market validation achieved.

So I'm not treating:

> ChatGPT thinks this is hilarious.

as meaningful evidence of anything.

Maybe nobody watches it.

Maybe five people watch it.

Maybe I watch it three times and decide it is stupid.

Fine.

There is a simpler test.

**Does making it entertain me?**

Because I already have the blog.

I already have the stories.

I already have Lucifer.

I already have a giant dog.

I apparently already have the hair.

## Castlevania, But It's My Fucking Life

So maybe I make one.

Not a season.

Not a content strategy.

Not a brand initiative.

One scene.

Lucifer in the yard.

Prestige gothic lighting.

Thirty-four meters of cable.

The Ridgeback silently judging the entire operation.

A clothes rack falls over in the background.

Lucifer doesn't even turn around.

He has accepted that part of the universe.

He reaches the gate.

Looks back toward the house.

The cable stretches across the entire property.

Music swells like Dracula is about to invade Wallachia.

Lucifer looks directly at the camera.

> "Who the fuck knows what a meter is?"

Black screen.

Title card.

**HERE WE FUCKING ARE.**

Yeah.

I might actually make this.
