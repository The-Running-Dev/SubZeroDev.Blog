# The 130-Kilo Tough Guy Defeated by Dog Shit

My former bar partner weighed 130 kilos.

There was physical intimidation. There were threats. There was a substantial amount of man available to support the suggestion that fucking with him would be a mistake.

Then there was the dog shit.

The dog shit proved too much.

<!-- truncate -->

I would like to give that particular opponent its due.

No muscles. No reputation. No need to establish dominance. A pile of dog shit simply existing as dog shit, and somehow the whole production of toughness encountered a serious technical difficulty.

I find that fucking funny.

Not because being disgusted is a character defect. Dog shit is disgusting. I am delighted that we can agree on something so fundamental without having to appoint a committee.

It is the contrast that gets me.

Here was someone whose threats and physical presence were part of how I experienced him. A man who could make his size relevant to another person's day. And here was a task for which being large and intimidating offered absolutely fuck all.

The shit did not care.

There is a useful distinction hiding inside the joke. Making someone take you seriously through intimidation is one kind of power. Being willing to deal with something unpleasant is a different capacity altogether.

One does not automatically come bundled with the other.

You can have 130 kilos of physical presence and still find that the unpleasant little thing in front of you has defeated the enterprise.

Weight is not a universal qualification.

Neither is bravado.

I know how tempting it is to turn a person into one clean verdict. Big threatening man meets dog shit; therefore the man is a coward in every conceivable circumstance. Nice and tidy. Also more than this story establishes.

What I know is that this particular piece of macho presentation looked ridiculous beside this particular pile of shit.

That is plenty.

I don't need to invent a grander humiliation. The contrast supplies its own.

Think of all the labor that goes into appearing formidable. The message that other people should be careful around you. The implication that you possess reserves of force which it would be unwise to test.

Then the test has no interest in force.

It requires dealing with something revolting.

Suddenly all that impressive equipment is irrelevant. There is no person to threaten into making the problem cease to be a problem. There is just the thing that needs doing and your willingness to do it.

An entire department of intimidation, and nobody qualified for sanitation.

That is the bit I remember.

I am not telling this story to claim I have never recoiled from anything disgusting. I am telling it because the gap between the image and the moment was so fucking good.

The enormous tough guy. The threats. The physical intimidation.

The dog shit.

You could write an elaborate satire of masculine posturing and still struggle to improve on the casting.

One participant had 130 kilos behind him.

The other was a pile of dog shit.

You already know which one proved too much for the other.
