# Yogurt With Salt

I am sitting in Bulgaria.

I am talking to a Bulgarian woman in a mixture of Bulgarian and English about language, identity, spies, and where I am "originally" from.

And while all of this is happening, I am eating yogurt with salt.

This is somehow the most accurate part of the entire evening.

<!-- truncate -->

Yogurt is Bulgarian.

The salt is mine.

That distinction matters.

Because if I simply said:

> "I'm sitting here eating Bulgarian yogurt."

Fine.

Normal.

Nationally compliant.

Nothing to investigate.

But no.

I put salt in it.

Not because this is some ancient village tradition passed down through generations.

Not because my grandmother taught me.

Not because there is a regional school of aggressively seasoned dairy that historians have somehow failed to document.

I just like salt in yogurt.

That's it.

## Bulgarian Enough

This is what made the timing so fucking funny.

I had just been asked where I am originally from.

That question is complicated enough that I answered:

> "Classified information."

Then the conversation moved through English and Bulgarian.

We joked about getting lost in every translation.

The spy thing appeared.

And through all of it, I am sitting there eating something completely Bulgarian in a way that is completely mine.

Yogurt.

Salt.

Done.

No identity committee required.

## The Modification

I do this with a lot of things.

Not as a philosophy.

Not as a statement.

I am not standing over the yogurt declaring independence from conventional dairy consumption.

I open the container.

I add salt.

I eat it.

That is the entire ideological platform.

But if someone asks why, now we apparently have material.

Because the yogurt itself belongs perfectly in the environment.

The modification does not.

It is not dramatic enough to make me foreign.

It is not traditional enough to make it Bulgarian.

It is just one tiny preference sitting on top of something familiar.

Which is a much better description of the moment than anything I could have produced in response to:

> "Where are you originally from?"

## No Translation Required

The dating conversation required translation.

"Very strong Friday night" required translation.

"More than a bottle of wine" somehow required quantitative analysis.

"Where are you from?" required classified clearance.

Yogurt with salt required nothing.

Spoon.

Salt.

Yogurt.

Problem solved.

No language barrier.

No biography.

No taxonomy.

No fucking spy investigation.

## Yogurt With Salt

There is probably no grand conclusion here.

I was not having a cultural revelation.

I was hungry.

I ate yogurt.

I put salt in it because I like salt in it.

Meanwhile, on my phone, two people were trying to figure out which language they could misunderstand each other in most efficiently.

And that is the image worth keeping.

A Bulgarian village.

A multilingual dating conversation.

A complicated answer to a simple question.

A completely ordinary tub of yogurt.

And one unnecessary but absolutely correct addition.

Salt.
